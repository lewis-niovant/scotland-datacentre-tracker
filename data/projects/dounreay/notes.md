# Dounreay — research notes (2026-07-30)

## What this is
An Invest Highland (Highland Council inward-investment agency) marketing listing, not a development project. The data-centres sector page and the investment-opportunities listing describe 148 ha at Dounreay, Caithness, owned by the Nuclear Decommissioning Authority, availability "short term", investment type "energy, data centre".

## What the marketing claims
- Power: "abundant clean and green power options" from the area around the decommissioned nuclear facility. No MW, no grid connection, no generation source named. Dounreay itself generates no power (reactors shut; site is being decommissioned).
- Fibre: subsea cable link to "Farice" (i.e. the FARICE-1/IRIS Iceland–Faroes–Scotland system landing in Caithness) giving "direct connection to London", and "potentially AE-5 in the future".
- Land: NDA ownership; no red-line boundary published; individual project page (/project/dounreay/) redirects back to the listing.

## What could not be verified
- No developer, operator or investor engagement found.
- No planning record: PlanIt search of Highland for "data centre" returns only 20/01258/SCRE (Mey — see meygen-caithness), a 2019 Inverness Campus cabin application, and a 2010 Inverness Marina application. Nothing at Dounreay.
- NDA's own position on releasing land for this use.

## Assessment
Status strategic_opportunity, M0, verification unverifiable, confidence low. Coordinates settlement-level (Dounreay site, ~58.578, -3.744).
