## Location refinement, 30 July 2026

Coarsening lifted by the project owner, and the 177 Bothwell Street attribution verified. DataVita's own Glasgow location page states that DV2 is purpose built, of Tier III design, and situated in the basement of 177 Bothwell Street, with 1,000 m2 of space and 130 racks; the 1,000 m2 figure is now recorded as `gross_floor_area_m2` (developer-stated), with a note that it is the data centre floorspace inside the basement and not the floor area of the 13-storey host building.

The building postcode is G2 7ER. This is corroborated independently by HFD Group Holdings Limited's registered correspondence address at Companies House and by NHS Education for Scotland's published office address in the same building. One commercial property listing (LoopNet) gives G2 7JH instead; that is noted in `land_notes` as an unresolved minor conflict but was not used. Coordinates were reset to the postcode-unit centroid for G2 7ER from postcodes.io (55.861447, -4.265494) and `location_precision` set to `exact`, since the street address is verified and the facility sits within a single identifiable building.

Still unknown: the year DV2 opened, whether the ~1 MW figure is IT load or gross power, and formal certification status. No planning application has been recorded. Consents exist for the 177 Bothwell Street office development itself, but that is the host building rather than the data centre, and nothing was found that identifies the basement data centre specifically; attributing the office consent to DV2 would misstate the record.
