# Brightsolid Aberdeen - research notes

Included as the individually notable Aberdeen operating facility (main commercial colocation site for the north-east; first Microsoft Azure Stack-enabled data centre in Scotland per Silicon UK coverage).

## What was checked
- DCD news (Tier III opening), Brightsolid Data Hall 1 technical specification PDF (2017), Silicon UK announcement coverage (GBP5m plan), Baxtel/Data Center Map listings (Whitemyres Ave).

## Conflicts
- Timeline: 2015 announcement coverage suggested an April 2015 go-live, but the DCD opening story and the 2017 tech-spec date support a 2017 opening; recorded as 2017 with first_public_date 2015 (announcement year approximated to 2015-01-01).
- Capacity: 1 MW IT (Hall 1) vs 2 MW total design across two halls; both recorded.

## Location refinement, 30 July 2026

Coarsening lifted by the project owner. Two publicly listed addresses exist for this facility and both are preserved rather than reconciled silently. Data Center Map lists it on Whitemyres Avenue, Aberdeen AB16 6HQ; PeeringDB (facility 2417, operator Brightsolid Online Technology Ltd) gives Lang Stracht, Aberdeen AB15 6DF. The postcode centroids returned by postcodes.io are only about 120 m apart (57.153087, -2.157559 and 57.15303, -2.159427), and Whitemyres Avenue runs off Lang Stracht within the same DC Thomson / Aberdeen Journals complex, so these are almost certainly two descriptions of one site. AB16 6HQ was preferred as the more specific street-level listing and coordinates were set to its centroid.

`location_precision` is `site_centroid`, not `exact`: no building number is published by any source, and the address conflict above is unresolved. No floor area, footprint or site area figure was found in a public source; the operator's Aberdeen data centre PDF could not be read (the fetched file returned no extractable text). Still unknown: building number, built-out capacity against the 2 MW two-hall design, and the scope of the Tier III certification. No planning application could be attributed. The Aberdeen City Council portal was unreachable from this environment on 30 July 2026, and although the facility was announced around 2015 and opened in 2017, no reference tying an application to Whitemyres Avenue or Lang Stracht was found, so nothing has been recorded.
