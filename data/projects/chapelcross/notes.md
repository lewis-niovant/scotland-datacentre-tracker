# Chapelcross (CX Technology Campus) — research notes

Researched 2026-07-30.

## What was checked
- **Dumfries & Galloway ePlanning portal** (eaccess.dumgal.gov.uk): search for "Chapelcross" attempted directly and via curl; blocked by a Cloudflare managed challenge. **No PAN, scoping or application reference could be verified against the official record.** This is the single biggest gap.
- **NDA/GOV.UK press release** (19 Nov 2025) and SOSE announcement: Heads of Terms between NDA and CX Power for 135 acres of unlicensed NDA land; "potential 1 GW data centre campus"; "several hundred high value jobs"; privately funded.
- **Developer website** (cxtechnologycampus.co.uk): 55 ha campus; up to 161,920 sqm data centre floorspace; EIA scoping report "submitted March 2026"; PPP targeted December 2026; phased construction 2027–2035; private-grid concept (wind/solar/biomass/hydrogen, private wire, hydrogen integration).
- **Local press (DnG24)**: consultation events 12–13 May 2026 (Annan/Creca) and 16–17 June 2026; "57 hectare campus… could be operational by 2030". Direct fetch of DnG24 returned 403; content taken from search excerpts.
- **Companies House**: CX POWER LIMITED, 16880627, incorporated **28 Nov 2025** — nine days *after* the announcement naming "CX Power". Registered in Camden, London; SIC 68209; directors Giovanni Maria Ciuccio and Graham Henry Edwards (the latter presumably of Global Mutual). PSC page showed no entries at retrieval. Link between this entity and the announced "CX Power" (GA Consultants + Global Mutual Properties Limited) is inferred, not evidenced by filings.
- **Chapelcross SSG meeting pack (18 Mar 2026)**: PDF download failed; search excerpts suggest the data centre was "relocated to the Chapelcross site" with manufacturing/R&D moving to Eastriggs and mention MoD/DSIT and "potential site commencement in 2027" — intriguing but unverified; follow up.
- **Grid**: no NESO queue entry, SPEN offer or any public grid record for a Chapelcross data centre connection found. SOSE ETZ marketing mentions a nearby Grid Supply Point.

## Provenance of the 1 GW figure
All instances trace to the 19 Nov 2025 NDA/CX Power announcement and developer marketing ("potential 1 GW data centre campus with a zero-carbon target"). It is undefined (IT load vs grid import vs campus energy), unsupported by any located grid or planning document, and framed against regional renewable *resource* (3 GW within 50 miles; 1.2 GW planned/consented) rather than contracted supply. Treated as developer_stated, low confidence.

## Conflicts preserved
- Site area: 55 ha (developer) vs 135 acres/54.6 ha (NDA/SOSE) vs 57 ha (DnG24).
- Timeline: "operational by 2030" (press) vs phased construction to 2035 (developer).
