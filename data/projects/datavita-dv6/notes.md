# DataVita/HFD DV6 (South Lanridge, Newhouse) — research notes (2026-07-30)

## Identity resolution (important)
The brief asked whether the "HFD Renewables NL data centre PAN 25/01328/PAN at South Lanridge Farm" is a project distinct from a "DV6 (Airdrie?)". It is **the same project**: APRS's site-by-site analysis states "This site is known as DV6", the developer group renamed SPV *HFD Renewables 2 Ltd* to **DataVita DV6 Ltd** (SC863396) in the same batch as DV4/DV7, and the PAN applicant address is HFD's 177 Bothwell Street office. Therefore no separate `hfd-south-lanridge` record was created; this record carries both names as aliases. It is **distinct from ILI's Aurelius** proposal ~0.9km away at North Lanrigg (`newhouse-aurelius`, cross-linked here and updated with this slug).

The "Airdrie" association in press stems from the gov.uk AIGZ release describing DataVita's "Airdrie site" — that is the existing DV1 campus (Chapelhall, Airdrie postal area), not DV6.

## Key facts
- PAN 25/01328/PAN received 17 Dec 2025 (same-day twin of DV4's 25/01322/PAN, identical description), Comments 29 Jan 2026 — the same day the Lanarkshire AI Growth Zone designation was announced (gov.uk / HLWS1290).
- Green Belt farmland, ward Fortissat, ML1 5ND; largest AIGZ site per APRS ("over 100 hectares" for the two largest sites combined).
- No EIA screening, no application, no site-specific MW figure as of 2026-07-30. Status: pending pre-application (M1).
- BESS: developer zone material suggests 2,400MWh (APRS elsewhere says 1,400MWh — inconsistency preserved); expected ECU s36 route if >50MW.
- 500MW is a zone-level claim; APRS's attribution of "most or all" of it to this site is recorded as inference only.
