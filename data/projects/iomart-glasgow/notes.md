Coarsening lifted by the project owner. iomart's DC1 address, 88 Middlesex Street, Glasgow G41 1EE, is advertised by the operator and appears in PeeringDB (facility 305, operator iomart Hosting Limited) and Data Center Map. Coordinates were reset to the postcode-unit centroid for G41 1EE from postcodes.io (55.85137, -4.282626). PeeringDB's independent geocode for the same facility is 55.851165, -4.283217, roughly 40 m away, so the two agree at building scale; `location_precision` is now `exact`.

No site area or floor area figure was added: directory listings quote about 8,267 sq ft and two data halls of roughly 4,000 sq ft each, but these describe data hall or white space rather than a building footprint or gross floor area, and no operator statement distinguishes them, so recording them under the site fields would have overstated what the sources say. Still unknown: opening year, IT versus gross power split, rack count, and the building's footprint. No planning application could be attributed: the Glasgow City Council Idox portal was not reachable from this environment on 30 July 2026 and no reference for this address appears in any secondary source. There are many unrelated applications in the Kinning Park area, so none has been recorded.
