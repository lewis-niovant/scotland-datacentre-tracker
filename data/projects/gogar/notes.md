## What was checked
- **CEC planning record via PlanIt** (citydev-portal blocks the research environment): keyword searches for `gogar`, `gogarburn`, `data centre`, `green data centre`, `redheughs`. Result: **no data centre application, PAN or screening at Gogar/Gogarburn**. All Edinburgh data centre cases are:
  - **Wester Hermiston / Riccarton (Apatura)** — 25/03978/PAN ("200MW demand utility capacity"), 25/02924/SCR **EIA Not Required 2025-07-04**, 25/05576/SCR **EIA Not Required 2026-05-06** (note: the existing `wester-hermiston` record shows this second screening as "under consideration" — PlanIt now shows it decided; suggested update for that record).
  - **1 Redheughs Avenue / South Gyle (Shelborn Drummond Ltd)** — 25/01968/PAN, 25/03575/SCR EIA not required, 25/04239/PPP refused Feb 2026, appeal PPA-230-2794.
- **APRS** "Calling for EIAs" page (fetched directly): calls the Shelborn scheme "**the Gogarburn data centre application**" and names "Shelborn Drummond's Request for an EIA screening opinion" — direct evidence that campaign/press usage of "Gogar(burn)" = the South Gyle scheme (Drummond House site adjoins the NatWest Gogarburn campus; RBS staff "relocated to Gogarburn" after its 2021 sale to Shelborn).
- **Foxglove Dec 2025 table**: no Gogar entry; only South Gyle (212.42 MW) and Wester Hermiston (200 MW) in Edinburgh.
- Press (Deadline News, Edinburgh Reporter, Edinburgh Inquirer — several 403-blocked, read via search extracts): all "huge Edinburgh data centre / EIA screened out" coverage maps onto those two schemes. Screening-opinion quote about "Gogar Special Landscape Area... and the Green Belt" appears in coverage of the Apatura screening.
- No evidence of any third scheme on NatWest Gogarburn land or the International Business Gateway.

## Conclusion (recorded honestly)
There is **no distinct Gogar data centre**. The SPICe "200 MW Gogar" line is a secondary conflation: exact-MW match points to **Wester Hermiston (Apatura)**; the "Gogar(burn)" naming convention in campaign/press material points to **South Gyle (Shelborn)**. Both are EIA-screened-out, so either fits SPICe's sentence. Record kept at M0 / status unknown / verification "reported", with `related_project_slugs` to both, to intercept future "Gogar" mentions.
