# Killellan / "Killean" AI Growth Zone — research notes (2026-07-30)

## Location resolved
The project is at **Killellan Farm, Toward, Dunoon PA23 7UJ, on the Cowal peninsula** (South Cowal, opposite Rothesay/Bute), not Kintyre. "Killean" (used by APRS) is a variant spelling that collides with the real Killean in Kintyre and Killellan House near Campbeltown — the council portal, Companies House and the developer all use "Killellan". Coordinates in `site.json` are an approximate placement (~55.877, -5.02) of the farm/quarry area between Toward and Ardyne from OSM; no red-line boundary has ever been published, so no `geo/boundary.geojson` is provided. DCD links the wider site narrative to the former Ardyne Point oil-rig yard.

## The claims-vs-evidence gap (core finding)
- **Only consents on the portal for the address**: `20/00229/PP` — "Erection of building for data storage purposes (Class 4)", applicant Mr R Mitchell, validated 6 Feb 2020, approved by delegated decision 15 May 2020 (no EIA, 6 comments); and `25/00445/CLAWU` — certificate of lawfulness for material commencement of that permission, approved 28 Mar 2025. The CLAWU (filed 20 Mar, decided 28 Mar 2025) kept the 2020 permission alive two months before the AI Growth Zone bid — a telling sequence.
- The task brief's "70×20 m building" figure was **not verified** from portal documents (documents tab not itemised in this pass); the description and Class 4 status confirm a single modest building.
- **No application, PAN, screening or scoping exists for the 74 ha campus.** The developer's own bid says a "comprehensive masterplan" would be submitted *after* AI Growth Zone designation. No evidence of UK Government designation or shortlisting was found.
- Grid evidence is limited to developer statements: 20 MW existing infrastructure claim, a 6 MW connection application for the first building, and a SPEN budget quote (20 MW, 33 kV, 16.8 km cable, £5.31m + VAT). Against this, marketing claims 100–600 MW phase 1, 2 GW+ build-out, and a "long-term vision" of 10 GW connectivity / 2–3 GW active.
- Fibre: Commsworld letter concedes diverse connectivity needs two **new** builds (Kilchattan Bay→Rothesay ~8 mi; Innellan exchange→site ~6 mi over terrain, landowner permissions needed).
- Renewables: 100%-renewable/island-mode claim rests centrally on a CorPower Ocean wave array (600 MW pre-FEED near Islay; 2 GW ambition) — pre-commercial technology, no marine licence located. Vanadium-flow batteries (Invinity), wind, solar unquantified.

## Corporate structure
- **Argyll Data Development Ltd** (SC759203, inc. 17 Feb 2023) and **Argyll Energy Development Ltd** (SC759139, inc. 16 Feb 2023), both registered at Killellan Farm. Directors: Peter Griffiths and Robert Lyle Mitchell (the landowner and the 2020/2025 planning applicant); Brian Bowen also a director of ADD. Two ADD directors appointed Aug 2024 resigned 1 Apr 2026.
- **Argyll Infrastructure Holdings Ltd** (SC870385) incorporated **17 Nov 2025** in Dunoon — press says AIH was "founded in 2023", contradicted by Companies House. AIH announced £15m raised (Dec 2025), £100m in negotiation, €2bn target with European Guarantee Services and Ocorian AIFM; three-year SambaNova exclusivity; Dunoon + Perth multi-site strategy. Investor identities undisclosed. PSC pages not parsed — follow-up.
- **Cowal Sand & Gravel Ltd.** (SC266441, inc. 2004, same address) operates the ~20 ha quarry (since 2006).

## SambaNova commitment
The Oct 2025 announcement is a **strategic partnership/marketing arrangement plus an equipment order and exclusivity with AIH**, not a construction or funding commitment for Killellan. The "UK sovereign AI cloud" that launched ~May 2026 is a **disaggregated platform hosted in multiple undisclosed existing UK data centres**, explicitly not at Killellan, which "remains under development".

## Conflicting figures preserved
- Investment: £15bn (Aug/Oct 2025 press) vs £6bn (developer's own May 2025 bid) vs £5.3bn capex (same bid).
- GVA: £734m/yr (Oct 2025 PR) vs £312m/yr over 10 years (May 2025 bid).
- Capacity: 100–600 MW → 2 GW+ (developer) vs "1000 MW scaling to 2000 MW" (APRS) vs 10 GW connectivity vision (bid).
