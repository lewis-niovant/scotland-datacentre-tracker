# Cockenzie data centre — research notes (2026-07-30)

## What this is
A council-led "strategic opportunity", not a planning case. East Lothian Council owns the former Cockenzie power station site (~92 ha, bought from ScottishPower in 2018) and is negotiating a joint venture with preferred bidder **Sustainable Development Capital LLP (SDCL, Companies House OC330266)** to deliver a hyperscale data centre on ~70 acres of the former **coal store** zone. The council would lease the land to the JV with **no capital contribution**; the council's financial return is not yet finalised. No planning application, PAN, screening or scoping was located on East Lothian's Idox portal via search coverage of news reporting (portal itself not directly searched — follow-up below).

## Timeline (redevelopment saga, brief)
- 1967/68–2013: Cockenzie coal-fired power station operates (~1,200 MW); 2015 chimneys demolished.
- 2017: council-funded masterplan vision; 2018: council buys the 92 ha site from ScottishPower.
- 2019–2021: planning permissions for offshore-wind substations; ~7 acres sold to Inch Cape Offshore Wind (substation operational from autumn 2025).
- 2022: NPF4 removes the thermal-generation designation constraint.
- 2023: £11.3m Levelling Up Fund round 2 award for remediation/site preparation; £100k masterplan refresh (Stantec 25-year masterplan; four zones: coastal, energy generation, coal store, Battle of Prestonpans).
- Feb 2025: competitive tender outcome agreed via **private report to Council** (content not public).
- 22 Oct 2025: SDCL announced as preferred supplier for a hyperscale data centre on the coal store; up to a year estimated to finalise legal agreements before statutory consultation.
- 25 Jun 2026: council update — JV negotiations progressing, 70-acre lease, community-benefits framework to be agreed, SDCL advisors doing pre-site due diligence (site characteristics, power availability, geological/hydrological surveys). Report on **main commercial terms expected August 2026**.

## Conflicts / tensions to preserve
- Council (McMillan) frames the deal as progress with training/jobs and long-term public financial return; Cllr Bruce (Con) calls it a "secretive deal" delivering "next to nothing" locally, contrasting <100 operational data centre jobs with ~3,300 permanent jobs projected in the masterplan, and alleges it breaks Levelling Up Fund commitments. Cllr McIntosh (Green) questions AI energy use and community-wealth alignment. Both community councils want scrutiny before any application; Prestonpans CC alleges "greenwashing".
- Dual role: the council is landowner, JV partner and planning authority — noted neutrally in organisations.json.

## Heritage context
The coal store zone sits beside the Battle of Prestonpans (1745) Inventory battlefield — one of the masterplan's four zones is explicitly the battlefield — and near the 1722 Tranent–Cockenzie Waggonway. No heritage body position on the data centre specifically was found (nothing yet to respond to, pre-application).
